# Pato

## Description

A yellow duck with a little green bucket hat, seen from the side and drawn in the eSheep style:
shaded pixel art with soft colored outlines.

He waddles bobbing his head, quacks, wags his tail and tries (and fails) to fly. The wind takes his
hat and it lands back on his head, crumbs fall and he pecks them all, he sits in a puddle and splashes,
kicks a ball, loses a balloon, sneezes a feather when a butterfly lands on his bill, catches a falling
star and gets a visit from a little blue bird on his hat.

## About pet

## Animations

* waddle with a head bob, run flapping, march, turn, sit, hop, somersault, fall and land
* quack, big quack, tail wag, tip hat, try to fly, windy hat, crumbs, worm, puddle splash, fish,
  ball, balloon, bubbles, butterfly, falling star, bird, sneeze, hiccup, laugh, dance, spin, tantrum
* reacts to the app: thinks, reads, types on a laptop and celebrates with Claude Code; drinks on the

* drag and toss with the mouse
