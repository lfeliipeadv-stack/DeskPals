# Tico

## Description

A toddler in pixel art, in a blue one-piece suit with a bear-ear hood.

He waddles around the screen with his head swaying, runs with his arms up, tiptoes, marches and
sits down kicking his feet. A ball rolls to his feet and he kicks it far away, his red balloon slips
away, he blows soap bubbles, follows a butterfly, jumps into a puddle, catches a falling star and
giggles when a little bird lands on his hood.

## About pet

Author: Luan Felipe

## Animations

* waddle, run, tiptoe, march, turn, sit and stand up, fall and land (or plop down on his bottom)
* ball, balloon, bubbles, airplane, butterfly, peekaboo, puddle jump, building blocks, toy drum,
  falling star, bird on his hood, toy car, crayon drawing, clap, cartwheel, hop
* sneeze, hiccup, laugh, dance, spin, teddy bear, tummy rumble, banana, apple, tantrum, ear wiggle
* reacts to the app: thinks, reads, types on a laptop and celebrates with Claude Code; drinks milk on the
  water reminder; umbrella, fan, scarf and snow cap with the weather; party at the end of a Pomodoro
* drag and toss with the mouse
