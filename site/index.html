    Features

    One little pet, lots to do.

    Everything is optional and stays off until you want it. Turn on only what makes sense for your day.

    It chats with you

    Hover over the pet and click Ask. The answer appears in a bubble above it.

    • With Ollama, the AI runs on your computer and nothing leaves it
    • Or with Gemini, Groq and OpenRouter, which have free plans
    • Remembers the conversation, has personalities and accepts voice dictation

    Reminds you to take breaks

    Water, stretching and breaks, Pomodoro with the pet quiet while you focus — and reminders written the way you'd say them to a friend:

    • “remind me to call Ana at 3pm”
    • “in 20 minutes”, “every day at 8”
    • Understood on your computer, no internet needed

    Warns you before meetings

    Connect your Google or Outlook calendar and the pet lets you know before each meeting. It also shows the weather forecast, warns you about rain and suggests a break after a long stretch without stopping.

    • With classic Outlook, it tells you how many emails arrived — never what they say
    • It's happy when you come back and celebrates the end of a long day

    Make your own pet

    The Pet Editor turns your images or a GIF into a pet that walks, turns, sleeps, falls and can be tossed — with a live preview of every animation.

    • New-pet wizard in three steps
    • Test on desktop drops the pet on your screen without touching what you already have
    • Export as .zip or publish to the gallery straight from the editor

    FAQ

    Quick questions.

    Is it really free?

    Yes, forever. DeskPals is open source under the MIT license: no paid version, no ads and no account.

    Will it slow down my computer?

    No. The app is about 3 MB and uses less than 1% of the CPU. In full-screen games or videos, the pet gets out of the way on its own.

    Does it work offline?

    Yes. The classic sheep comes built into the app, and pets you've already downloaded keep working. The internet is only used to download pets, languages and updates — and for AI or the weather forecast, if you turn them on.

    What about meetings?

    Ctrl + Alt + P hides all pets and sounds instantly. Press it again to bring them back. Do Not Disturb mode also keeps the pet quiet.

    Windows said the file is unrecognized. Is it safe?

    The warning appears because the files don't have a paid digital signature. Every release publishes the SHA-256 of all files so you can check them, and all the code is on GitHub. Click More info › Run anyway.

    Can I make my own pet?

    Yes. The Pet Editor turns your images or a GIF into a complete pet, and the Publish to gallery button shares it with everyone. See Make your pet (in Portuguese).

    How do I move everything to another computer?

    In Settings, Backup exports pets, reminders, notes and preferences into a single file. Automatic backup saves that file every day to a OneDrive or Google Drive folder.
